An artefact needing another artefact's definition, rationale, or substance cites it by relation and does not restate that content in its own words.
Where the citing artefact needs context to make the citation legible, it states only what points the reader toward the cited artefact, never the cited point itself.

## Rationale

A restated definition drifts from its home the moment the home changes, and nothing signals the drift; a citation cannot drift, because it holds nothing of its own to fall out of step.
This applies Single Source of Truth to the corpus's own prose about itself, not only to the systems the corpus describes.
