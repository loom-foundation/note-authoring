In a Note corpus, a Term's or a Persona's `name` is written in "Title Case", in frontmatter and in prose; every other kind's `name` is written in sentence case.

## Rationale

Terms and Personas are often used in prose without cross-references to their artefacts.

Title Case marks a defined Term or Persona apart from ordinary prose at a glance, with no tool needed; sentence case everywhere else keeps that mark meaningful.
