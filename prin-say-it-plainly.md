Write the plainest prose that is still precise; keep a distinction only where plain wording would lose it.

## Why this matters

Note corpora are read cold, by people and by AI agents.
Plain prose is understood by more readers, costs less attention, and takes fewer tokens to load.

## How to apply

- Prefer the short sentence, the common word, and the active voice.
- Break a sentence that carries more than one idea.
- Read the passage as a stranger would. If it needs decoding, rewrite it.

## Considered alternative

A richer register, with asides and flourishes: discarded.
The pleasure of ornament is not worth the misreadings and the reading cost it adds in technical writing.

## Origin

The plain-language tradition (the plain-English movement; the U.S. Plain Writing Act of 2010) and the technical-writing standard of clarity and brevity.
