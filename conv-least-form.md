A Note artefact takes the smallest form that carries its intent:
- **Title** is a concise label of at most eight words.
- **Optional metadata attributes and sections** earn their place only by carrying unique substance; anything else is filler.

## Rationale

Some agents, given a template, default to the opposite: they populate every optional field just because it exists.
